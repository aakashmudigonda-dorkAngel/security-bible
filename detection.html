Detection Basics

Quick refresher

Start with a detection hypothesis. Identify the required telemetry and fields, define the signal, test it with representative events, and make the alert actionable. Missing or delayed data can undermine an otherwise sound rule.

Worked example

For a privileged role assignment, record the actor, target, role, time, and change reference. Triage by checking whether the change matches an approved request and whether the account subsequently accessed sensitive data.

Interview practice

How would you tune a noisy detection?

Review false positives, measure volume, and add context such as approval records or asset criticality. Retest intended cases and check for blind spots before deploying.
