Threat Modeling

Quick refresher

Describe assets, actors, entry points, data flows, and where trust changes. Identify plausible threats, prioritize by likelihood and impact, and record mitigations with owners and verification steps.

Worked example

For a document-sharing app, draw the browser, API, document store, and identity provider. At the API-to-store boundary, consider whether the authorization policy consistently enforces tenant ownership.

Interview practice

How would you threat model a file-sharing feature in ten minutes?

Agree on scope and assets, sketch the data flow, identify trust boundaries, prioritize a few concrete failure scenarios, and propose controls with tests.
